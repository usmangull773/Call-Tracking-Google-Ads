Four steps, one phone call

Nothing complicated. Here is the path from a search to a call ringing at your shop.

1

Someone in your area searches for a plumber or a water heater fix.

2

Your ad sends them to a clean, fast page built for one thing: calling.

3

They tap the number. It is a tracking number, so the source is recorded.

4

The call forwards to your shop, recorded, and tagged plumbing or water heater.

That tag is the whole point. Plumbing calls and water heater calls stay separate, so you always know what is actually working.
